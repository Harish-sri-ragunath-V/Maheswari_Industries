How to Use Customer Features:

1 Search Products: Use the search bar to find specific furniture. Filter by category or search keywords.
2 View Gallery: Scroll down to see beautiful furniture images and design inspirations.
3 Ask Enquiry: Click "Enquire" on any product to ask questions directly.
4 Shop Items: Click "Add" to add products to your cart. Click cart icon to view and manage items.
5 Contact Us: Use the contact form to send messages or subscribe to newsletter.

How to Add Products:

1 Login as Admin: Click lock icon, use admin/admin123
2 Go to Products Tab: Click "Products" in the admin dashboard
3 Click Add Product: Fill in details - title, category, price
4 Upload Image: Select from your computer (not internet URL!)
5 Save Product: Click "Save" and product appears on store

How to Manage Gallery:

1 Go to Gallery Tab: Click "Gallery" in admin dashboard
2 Upload Image: Click "Upload Image" button
3 Add Title: Give the image a descriptive title
4 Select Image: Upload from your computer
5 View in Gallery: Image appears in public gallery section

How to Manage Customer Messages:

1 Go to Messages Tab: Click "Messages" in admin dashboard
2 View Enquiries: See all customer messages and product enquiries
3 Respond to Messages: Click "Responded" after replying to customer
4 Mark Status: Mark as Accepted, Responded, or Declined
5 Customer Contact Info: See email and phone to contact directly

Important Notes

Image Upload: Always upload images from your computer for better performance. Internet URLs may not work properly.
Data Storage: All data is stored in your browser. Clearing browser data will clear all saved information.
Responsive Design: Website works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Tips for Best Experience:

  • Use clear, descriptive product titles
  • Upload high-quality images for better appearance
  • Respond to customer enquiries promptly
  • Keep gallery updated with fresh images
  • Update contact information regularly

Troubleshooting

Can't login?

  • Check if cookies are enabled in your browser
  • Clear browser cache and try again
  • Try using incognito/private window
  • Verify credentials: admin/admin123

Images not showing?

  • Ensure image format is supported (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP)
  • Check browser console for errors (Press F12)
  • Try refreshing the page
  • Upload smaller sized images

Data not saving?

  • Check if browser storage is full
  • Clear browser cache and try again
  • Disable browser extensions that block storage
  • Try different browser if issue persists
